01 / About

A computational lens on other worlds.

My work connects observations with physical models: from transmission spectra of hot Jupiters to the long-term stability of moons in compact planetary systems. I am interested in the questions that live at the overlap of atmosphere, orbit, and computation.

02 / Research

Following the signals.

Three connected ways I investigate the physics of planets beyond our Solar System.

01

High-resolution spectroscopy · 2025—present

Young worlds, measured in motion.

Constraining the masses of exoplanets orbiting young stars through spectral feature analysis with ground-based spectroscopy. I generate high-resolution transmission spectra, run injection-recovery tests, and use cross-correlation analysis to isolate planetary signals.

With Prof. Andrew Vanderburg, Harvard University Collaborating with Dr. Saugata Barat, MIT
spectral trace
02

Orbital dynamics · 2024—present

The possible lives of exomoons.

I model the stability of potential moons around the TRAPPIST-1 planets, integrating their orbits across millions of periods to map the outer limits of where exomoons may persist.

With Dr. Sean N. Raymond, LAB Bordeaux
03

Planetary climate & chemistry · 2024—present

Building atmospheres from first principles.

I develop CLIMAX, a one-dimensional radiative-convective framework for planetary atmospheres, and contribute to XODIAC, a GPU-accelerated photochemical model with flexible reaction networks.

With Dr. Liton Majumdar, NISER Bhubaneswar
