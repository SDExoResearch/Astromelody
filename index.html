Biography

[cite: 2, 7] I am a computational astrophysicist and an alumnus of the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Kolkata[cite: 3]. Currently, I work as a Post-Masters Researcher at NISER Bhubaneswar under the supervision of Dr. Liton Majumdar[cite: 18, 19, 20].

[cite: 22, 23, 28, 32] My research centers heavily around mapping exoplanetary climates, exploring the chemical composition of atmospheres through parallelized photochemical codes, and testing multi-body structural stability across deep gravitational systems using N-body architectures.
